# Domain glossary

- **Employer**: The individual who directly employs support staff and manages the schedule and funding.
- **Support recipient**: The individual receiving support at home and on outings, whose appointments appear in the calendar.
- **Support staff**: The single role shared by all employees. Staff do not live in the home.
- **Coverage need**: A time window during which support is required, normally from one employee. It exists independently of any shift or assignment.
- **Shift block**: A planned interval of staff work that can be assigned to an employee.
- **Assignment**: The association of an employee with a shift block.
- **Overlap exception**: An explicitly approved period when more than one employee may provide support simultaneously.
- **Availability**: The periods during which an employee can be assigned, accounting for recurring restrictions and date-specific exceptions.
- **Preference**: A scheduling choice that is desirable but may be relaxed; distinct from a mandatory restriction.
- **Workweek**: Monday through Sunday in Ontario local time, used for weekly planning and funding.
- **Weekly budget**: The funding available for a workweek, including wages and employer costs, with no automatic carryover.
- **Scheduled cost**: Estimated wages and employer costs resulting from assignments; distinct from actual payroll expenditure.
- **Sleeping/on-call overnight**: Overnight coverage during which the employee may sleep but remains available to provide support. This term does not determine paid working time.
- **Appointment**: An event from one of the support recipient's external calendars, displayed alongside staff shifts.
- **Published schedule**: A draft deliberately accepted by the employer as the current working schedule.
